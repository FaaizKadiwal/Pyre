How to play
  • Everyone gets 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up cards on top of them, and 3 in hand. Before play you may swap hand cards with your face-up cards.
  • On your turn play a card, or several cards of the same rank, equal to or higher than the top of the pile. Suits do not matter. 3 is lowest, ace is highest. Your hand refills to 3 while the deck lasts.
  • 2 can be played on anything, and anything can be played on a 2.
  • 10 can be played on anything and burns the pile. You play again.
  • Four of a kind on top of the pile also burns it, and the player who completed it plays again.
  • Cannot or do not want to play? Pick up the whole pile. The next player starts a new one.
  • Once your hand and the deck are empty, play your face-up cards. If you have to pick up, one face-up card goes with the pile. Then flip your face-down cards blind; a bad flip means you pick up the pile.
  • Whoever gets rid of everything first is safe. The last player left holding cards is the shithead.
  • Modes: Classic is the plain game with a minute a turn. Party adds the house rules: threes skip, sevens force a 7 or lower, eights are see-through, nines reverse, and two jokers that go on anything and reverse. Blitz is Classic on a 15-second clock. Inferno is everything on with 20 seconds. The host can fine-tune any of it in the room.
  • If the turn timer runs out, the game plays for you. Lose two turns in a row and the table plays for you until you act again; the same happens while you are disconnected.
  • Keyboard: 1–9 select a card, Enter plays the selection or presses Ready, P picks up, Esc clears.
